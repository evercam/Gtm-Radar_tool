Source Hub — who gets which leads

Answer these and download a configuration file. An admin uploads it in Settings → Configuration, reviews what it would change, and applies it. Nothing changes until they confirm.

This covers territories, the team, vertical specialisation and daily capacity. It does not touch scoring, enrichment, source schedules or sales/marketing routing — those are left exactly as they are.

1

Which territories are you selling into?

Each one becomes a rule. Leads outside every selected territory stay in the unassigned pool.

Stored as slugs — usa, ireland — not as the codes you see on screen (US, IE). The file uses the slugs, so the rules actually match.

2

Who receives leads?

No account or invitation needed — a name is enough for someone to own a lead. An email also lets the Apollo export put their name on the contact.

NameEmailRoleTerritoryPer day

Per day is a hard ceiling: once someone hits it, assignment skips them until tomorrow. The team’s ceilings added together are the most leads that can be handed out in a day, whatever the lead mix says.

3

Does anyone specialise?

Leave someone blank and they can receive any vertical. That is usually what you want to start with.

A specialisation is a hard limit, not a preference: a lead outside someone’s verticals is never assigned to them. If every person is specialised, anything outside those verticals goes unassigned indefinitely.
4

What should a day’s leads look like?

Assigning strictly by score sounds fair and is not — whichever vertical scores highest takes the whole day.

A safety limit on top of each person’s ceiling. Useful the first week, when you want to see what assignment does before it does it a thousand times.
